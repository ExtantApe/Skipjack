First version, for Paper 26.1 and up.

- Press jump in a boat or raft to hop over a block or an ice ridge. Sideways speed and damage don't change.
- Boats can hop from land, and on land go about their water speed by default (`land-speed`).
- A cooldown, and a cap on how high a chain of hops can climb.

Tested: unit tests, and bots on a copy of a real server on Paper 26.2 and 26.3 build 249. Not yet tried in a real game client, so the hop height may need tuning.
